# Brag Plan: Bold

## What is this app?
Bold is a Spanish-language workspace that brings daily priorities, projects, tasks, reports, permissions, and organizational control into one coherent product.

## The angle
The film moves from visual noise to a single calm command center. Its specific claim is not generic productivity: Bold lets a team see what matters today, turn it into accountable work, and read the result without leaving the workspace.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
On a charcoal field, scattered fragments of work briefly drift out of alignment. A sharp red point locks them into the Bold wordmark while the real product line appears: **“Todo tu trabajo, en un solo lugar.”** The first transformation earns the reveal.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A recreated Bold Inicio dashboard assembles from real product widgets: Indicadores, Mis tareas, Proyectos, and Tareas por estado.
- A cursor adds **“Preparar presentación de clientes”**; the task lands in the Lanzamiento Q4 board, moves to **En curso**, and advances project progress from 68% to 74%.
- The product pulls back into an executive snapshot: progress, completed work, and the **Seguridad y acceso** signal share one composed frame.

## Outro / punchline
The interface recedes into the Bold mark. Final line: **“Trabajo claro. Equipos en movimiento.”** A small red point lands last, followed by **“Conoce Bold.”**

## User flow worth showing
Enter the daily dashboard → create and advance a real task → see project progress and organizational visibility update.

- Creative direction: quiet premium product film with decisive red accents
- Interpretation: clean feature-forward scenes, generous holds, precise UI choreography, and restrained copy; motion provides energy while typography stays calm.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm modern business bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, confident and clean at approximately 114.84 BPM
- Music treatment: begin at track start under the hook, keep below the product, support the central interaction, and fade smoothly through 20.8–21.0s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read; target 3.70s for the dashboard reveal, 8.44s for task creation, 12.65s for the progress result, and 17.91s for the final Bold lockup; use the 9.50/10.54/11.60/12.65 beat window for the interaction sequence
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the red point and progress fills may breathe by only a few percent with music energy
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched; one soft UI click, one gentle task-drop cue, one restrained completion accent, one dry logo tick
- Audio-coupled moments: scattered fragments lock to the wordmark; cursor click creates the task; task transfer lands on a beat; progress counts upward; final red point lands with the logo tick
- Restraint rule: no whooshes on every transition, no loud impacts, and no sound that competes with readability

### Scene 3 — Work moves — 7.4s
